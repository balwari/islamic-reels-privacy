Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Salafi Reels (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Salafi Reels mobile application (also listed as Islamic Reels) and the website at www.salafireels.com.

1. Who we are

Salafi Reels is a short-form Islamic video platform. For privacy questions, contact support@salafireels.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

  • Name, username, email address, and optional mobile number
  • Password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we do not store plaintext passwords)
  • Profile photo and bio, if you add them
  • Preferred reel languages
  • Sign-in via Google, if you choose Google login (we receive basic profile details Google provides, such as name, email, and profile photo)

App activity

  • Videos you watch, like, save, share, or comment on
  • Accounts you follow
  • Search queries within the app
  • Upload access requests and videos you upload (if permitted)
  • Reports you submit about content or comments
  • Contact / support messages you send through the app or email

Device and technical data

When you register or use the app, we may collect limited device and app information (for example device type, OS version, app version, and related technical metadata) to secure accounts, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. We also process IP address and similar network data through our hosting and authentication providers.

Website

Visiting this website may involve standard server and CDN logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) used for security, performance, and operations.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the app and website (feeds, profiles, comments, uploads, deep links)
  • Authenticate you and protect accounts (including rate limits and abuse prevention)
  • Personalize content, such as language preferences
  • Moderate content and enforce our rules (including blocking accounts when necessary)
  • Respond to support requests and contact messages
  • Measure basic usage (for example view counts) to operate the product
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as needed to run the service:

  • Infrastructure providers — for example database, authentication, file storage, and content delivery partners that process data on our behalf
  • Other users — public profile details (such as username, name, avatar) and content you publish (reels, comments) are visible to other users according to product features
  • Legal / safety — when required by law or to protect users, rights, and security

5. Data retention

We keep account and content data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. If your account is deactivated or blocked, some data may be retained for security, moderation, and legal reasons. You may request deletion by contacting support@salafireels.com.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards such as encrypted transport (HTTPS), authenticated API access, and secure password handling through our auth provider. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children’s privacy

Salafi Reels is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Your choices

  • Update profile details in the app
  • Control language preferences for your feed
  • Sign out or stop using the app
  • Request account or data deletion via support@salafireels.com
  • If you signed in with Google, you can also manage Google account permissions in your Google settings

9. International processing

Our providers may process data in data centers outside your country. By using Salafi Reels, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in those locations with appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app or website after changes means you accept the updated policy.
